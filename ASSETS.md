All fighter art (`client/public/art/**`: SVGs, blockout PNGs, parts JSON) is generated by
`scripts/art-gen.mjs` and `scripts/blockout.mjs` — our own work, no third-party source.
The 3D sprite atlases, the arena ground, the arena props and the lagoon water tile (`scripts/art3d.mjs`,
`scripts/art-arena.mjs`) are rendered in Blender from our own procedural models/shaders — no vendored kits so far.
